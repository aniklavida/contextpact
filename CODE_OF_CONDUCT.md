# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, constructive and specific.
- Welcome different experience levels and perspectives.
- Focus criticism on ideas and evidence, not people.
- Accept responsibility, apologise and repair harm when appropriate.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualised attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through GitHub. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation or ban contributors when necessary to protect the community.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
